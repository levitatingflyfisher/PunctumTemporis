Capture one second of video every day and compile them into montage films — the private, offline, own-your-footage alternative to a subscription cloud video diary.

• A daily clip, and a calm nudge if you miss one
• On-device face-crop, so people stay centred
• Montage compilation on your phone, not a server
• Your videos are included in backups, so a phone change does not cost you the year
• Retro aesthetic, because a video diary should feel like something

No accounts, no telemetry, no upload. The APK asks for no network permission at all — your footage physically cannot leave this way.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
